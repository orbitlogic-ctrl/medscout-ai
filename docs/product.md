# Product

MedScout aims to help people discover and compare healthcare services using evidence about facilities and their offerings.

Milestone 1 provides demonstration data for Bologna, Modena, Parma, Reggio Emilia, and Ferrara. It includes dermatology consultations, cardiology consultations, and abdominal ultrasounds. The city and region labels describe the geographic scope. The facilities and offerings are fictional, synthetic, and unverified.

Prices, appointment availability, real provider verification, and patient data are outside this milestone.
